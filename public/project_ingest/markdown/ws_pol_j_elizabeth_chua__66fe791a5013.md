WS POL-J: Students with Disabilities and Human Decision Safeguards

IEP and 504 Plans must be developed by qualified professionals. Consistent with FERPA, student information or PII may not be put into a generative AI tool.  Staff may use generative AI as a starting point to brainstorm about potential goals and accommodations, but staff cannot rely solely on this output.  All IEP and 504 plans must be reviewed, edited, and customized by human staff.

AI and AI tools may be used for accessible materials, but must be reviewed by a human before distribution and special attention must be given for biases in AI generated materials.
