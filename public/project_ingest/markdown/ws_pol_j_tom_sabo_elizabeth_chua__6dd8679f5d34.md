# WS-POL-J — Students with Disabilities and Human Decision Safeguards

Protect Students With Disabilities and Multilingual Learners
Ensure AI use does not conflict with IEPs, 504 Plans, or language supports. Legitimate uses such as translation, text-to-speech, or paraphrasing for access should not be broadly prohibited through AI policies or integrity rules.

Rationale: Concerns were raised that blanket bans or rigid integrity rules may unintentionally penalize students who rely on assistive or language supports. Equity requires distinguishing misuse from legitimate access needs. Possible Implications:

Require Special Education and English Learner expertise in AI policy review and/or…

Prohibit AI guidelines that negate approved accommodations without formal review – this could be an overarching rule when adopting AI related policy.
