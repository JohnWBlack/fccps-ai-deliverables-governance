WS POL-E: Staff and Teacher Use of AI

FCCPS values the expertise of its staff and teachers and the use of AI and other technology must be aligned with our values and goals.  The use of AI and AI tools, especially GenAI, is meant to augment, but not replace the professional expertise of staff and teachers.

When staff and teachers consider using AI, GenAI, and AI tools, they must consider both the risks and the benefits and the benefits must outweigh the risks. Risks and benefits should be considered for the students, the staff and teachers, and society.   Use must keep equity and inclusion in mind, particularly related to collection of biometric and behavioral data.

Before using AI, GenAI, and AI tools with students, staff and teachers must be trained appropriately. FCCPS will provide training and promote AI Literacy among teachers and staff.

AI, GenAI, and AI tools may be used to enhance and support teaching and learning.  Educators must keep in mind the IB Leaner profile and use their judgment about the risks and benefits of using AI in a given situation.  Draft IB principles are here (https://www.ibo.org/news/news-about-the-ib/ib-ai-principles/).

Teachers may use AI to brainstorm for ideas related to lesson planning and material, and human review and ownership is required before usage. AI should be used for increasing effectiveness and student learning outcomes rather than cognitive offloading.  Promoting critical thinking and creativity should be prioritized over AI usage.

Staff may use AI and AI tools for operations and resource planning, scheduling, and summarizing non-sensitive information.

AI and AI tools may be used for translation and creation of accessible materials, but must be reviewed by a human before distribution.
