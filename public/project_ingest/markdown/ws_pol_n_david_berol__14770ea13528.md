# WS POL N David Berol

WS-POL-N Workforce & Budget Implications

AI adoption may have an adverse long-term impact on faculty working conditions and availability of human-based educational services in Falls Church City.  Increasing the scope of teacher responsibilities, concurrent with providing AI tools that purport to make teachers more efficient, risks amounting to “AI replacing teachers,” or at least being perceived as such.

To mitigate these concerns, the Board should reassure the community that it would be inappropriate to cite AI-driven efficiency gains as a justification to:

Reduce the teacher workforce.

Delay the backfill of faculty vacancies.

Increase the scope of teacher responsibilities.
