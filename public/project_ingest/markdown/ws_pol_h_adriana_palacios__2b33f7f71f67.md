# WS-POL-H — Public Accountability, Notice, Consent, and Feedback

## Decision 11 — Committee Perspective (No Full Consensus)

The committee generally agrees that transparency, communication, and accountability are essential to building trust in the use of AI in FCCPS. There is broad alignment that students and families should understand how AI is used and how it affects learning and student experience.

The committee also recognizes that AI tools are already widely accessible outside of school environments and are increasingly part of students’ everyday reality. As a result, communication should not focus solely on restrictions or risk mitigation, but also on helping families understand how AI can be used responsibly to support learning.

There are important tradeoffs regarding how these commitments are implemented. These include balancing clarity and transparency with administrative burden, ensuring consistency across classrooms while preserving educator flexibility, and determining when consent or opt-out mechanisms are appropriate versus impractical.

The committee recommends that FCCPS adopt a clear, accessible, and practical approach to public accountability that builds trust and shared understanding across the community.

## Commitments to Consider

### Clear and Accessible Communication

The committee recommends that FCCPS provide clear, plain-language explanations of how AI is used in instructional and operational contexts. Communication should avoid technical jargon and be designed so that students and families can easily understand what AI is being used for, when it is being used, and what it means for their experience.

Communication should be accessible across languages and community backgrounds. This includes providing translated materials and using multiple communication channels to ensure information reaches all families.

Communication should also help families understand both the opportunities and the risks of AI use, so that expectations are not driven by uncertainty or fear, but by shared understanding.

### Notice and Transparency

The committee recommends that families and students receive appropriate notice when AI tools are used in ways that meaningfully affect learning, evaluation, or student experience.

Examples of such use may include:

AI use in classroom instruction

AI-supported feedback or assessment

AI systems that process student data

The level of notice should be proportional to the level of impact. Overly complex or universal notification requirements may reduce clarity and create unnecessary burden without improving understanding.

### Consent and Opt-Out Considerations

The committee recognizes that in certain higher-risk contexts, including those involving sensitive data or meaningful impact on student experience, it may be appropriate to consider consent or opt-out mechanisms.

At the same time, the committee emphasizes that overly rigid consent structures may be difficult to implement consistently and may unintentionally limit access to learning opportunities.

Where opt-out options are provided, clear and equitable alternatives should be defined to ensure that students are not disadvantaged.

## Communication, Transparency, and Family Engagement

The committee emphasizes that policy expectations must be clearly and consistently communicated to students and families.

Students should receive clear guidance about when AI use is:

permitted

conditionally allowed

or restricted

Inconsistent expectations across classrooms may create confusion and undermine both learning and accountability. FCCPS should support alignment while allowing appropriate flexibility for educators.

The committee also emphasizes the importance of engaging families as partners. Communication should support families in understanding how AI is being used to support learning, not only how it is being controlled.

## Accountability Mechanisms

### Public Accountability and Governance

The committee recommends that FCCPS maintain a public-facing explanation of its approach to AI use. This may include:

the types of AI tools in use

the purposes they serve

the safeguards in place

Clear ownership should be established for oversight, including responsibility for:

reviewing AI tools

responding to concerns or incidents

updating guidance as needed

### Feedback and Community Engagement

The committee recommends that FCCPS provide accessible channels for students, families, and staff to raise questions, concerns, or feedback related to AI use.

This may include:

a public feedback mechanism

opportunities for community input

structured ways to surface issues during implementation

Feedback should be reviewed and used to inform ongoing improvements, not only collected.

## Implementation Considerations

The committee recognizes that accountability requirements must be practical to implement. Overly complex communication, notice, or consent structures may create burden for staff and reduce effectiveness.

Policy should define expectations at a high level, while allowing implementation details—such as communication formats, tools, and timelines—to be handled at the administrative level.
