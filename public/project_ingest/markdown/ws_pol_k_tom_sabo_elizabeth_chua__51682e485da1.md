# WS-POL-K — Protection of Student Privacy, Data Governance, and Vendor/Procurement Governance

Require Equity-Centered Vendor Transparency
Mandate that AI vendors disclose training data limitations, bias testing practices, multilingual and accessibility support, and data use and retention policies as a condition of approval or procurement.

Recommendation: Mandate that vendors disclose, at a minimum:

## Training data sources and known limitations

Bias testing practices

Multilingual and accessibility capabilities; and limitations therof

Data usage, retention, and opt-out options

Ties back to a key concern in #1 above: Data mining around things like detecting at risk students and determining disciplinary actions that can disproportionately harm marginalized students

Rationale: Vendor opacity poses equity, privacy, and trust risks—especially for children. Without transparency, FCCPS cannot fulfill its equity obligations.
