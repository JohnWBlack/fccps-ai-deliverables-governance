SUMMARY

Meeting 5 focused on advancing the committee’s role as an advisory body to the School Board, with emphasis on how to structure and communicate policy recommendations without overstepping into implementation or operational detail.

# Key alignment points emerged:

The committee’s role is to surface well-reasoned recommendations, including areas of agreement and disagreement, rather than produce a finalized operational policy.

The School Board will translate recommendations into formal policy and regulation, reinforcing the importance of clarity, coherence, and traceability in committee outputs.

There is tension between specificity and flexibility, particularly in areas such as grade-banded AI use, disclosure expectations, and implementation feasibility.

# Major themes discussed:

## Policy Framing vs. Drafting

Some members supported providing consensus-based policy language.

Others emphasized the importance of capturing dissent and tradeoffs explicitly, rather than forcing convergence.

## Grade-Banded AI Use

Strong agreement on the importance of developmentally appropriate AI usage.

Divergence on whether AI should function as a co-teacher, particularly for younger students.

Recognition that operational decisions (e.g., grade band rules) will ultimately sit with the School Board.

## Human vs. AI Roles in Learning

Emphasis on ensuring students understand the difference between AI-generated and human-generated content.

Discussion of the need for students to develop cognitive independence (“cognitive pipeline”) alongside AI literacy.

## Implementation Boundaries

Clear distinction reinforced between:

Committee → recommendations

School Board → implementation and regulation

Need to avoid over-specifying operational details while still providing actionable guidance.

## Document Structure and Process

Agreement to move toward a structured, shared document with defined ownership.

Timeline pressure acknowledged, with a push to:

Finalize structure quickly

Assign sections

Deliver a coherent draft for the next meeting
