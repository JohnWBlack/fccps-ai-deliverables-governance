# Chair notes for Tina

## Opening frame

“Good morning. Today is not about forcing agreement or finalizing policy language. Our job is to help the School Board by organizing what this committee wants them to know: where we align, where there are trade-offs, and where there is meaningful dissent.”

## Why this meeting is different

You can say:
“Meeting 4 surfaced a lot of useful material, but also showed that trying to settle every principle or definition in-meeting is slowing us down. The Board has made clear that unanimity is not required and that disagreement itself can be useful input.”

## Ground rules

“Today we are using discussion to sort and clarify, not to win. If you have a minority or dissenting view, we want it captured. We do not need to debate every point to closure.”

## On grade bands

If discussion gets stuck on exact cutoffs:
“We are using grade bands as a discussion lens, not trying to settle exact breakpoints for the School Board. The useful product is the considerations for younger vs. older students.”

## On written inputs

If members say their submission was incomplete:
“That is fine. Partial written input is still useful. Today’s goal is to organize and improve what we have.”

## On the draft risk register

“This is a first-pass sorting document, not a vote. We are using it to see what clearly belongs in the Board-facing package, what needs to be merged, and what still needs work.”

## When discussion starts to spiral

Use one of these:

“Let’s capture that and keep moving.”

“That sounds like a trade-off the Board should weigh.”

“That may be a dissenting view worth preserving rather than resolving here.”

“Let’s not get stuck on exact wording today.”

## Equal airtime move

If one voice starts to dominate:
“Let’s pause and hear one brief reaction from others before we go deeper.”

## Bucket-sorting prompt

For each topic:
“Is this something we broadly align on, something we want to frame as a trade-off, or something where we want to preserve distinct viewpoints?”

## Closing frame

“What we are building is not a final policy. It is a Board input package: recommendations, considerations, trade-offs, and dissent where it matters.”
