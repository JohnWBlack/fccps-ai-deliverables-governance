WS POL-F: Student Use – PreK-5

AI usage will follow the individual schools, with age-appropriate usage (or lack thereof) and education by schools and grade band (PreK, K-2, 3-5).  Usage of AI and Learning of AI must promote safe, ethical, and developmentally appropriate integration of AI in early childhood and elementary education.

The primary focus in early childhood and elementary education should be on AI Literacy that prioritizes understanding how AI works.  FCCPS should consider existing frameworks (e.g., AI4K12, UNESCO) for developmentally appropriate pedagogy for AI Literacy going from more concrete to more abstract concepts.

Students in PreK and K-2 shall not use GenAI tools at school independently and will not be asked to create individual accounts.  Any AI usage must be through age-appropriate, and teacher-led platforms. These activities must be vetted for safety and ethics. AI usage in students in grades 3-5 must also be through age-appropriate, and teacher-led platforms and students must disclose when AI tools are used in a project.
