# WS POL B David Berol

WS-POL-B Adoption vs. Restriction Posture:

Three viewpoints guide our overall approach:

We concur with the 2026 assessment from the Brookings Institute that “at this point in its trajectory, the risks of utilizing AI in education overshadow its benefits.”

We believe that Generative AI (GenAI) tools are already used widely throughout the FCCPS community. Accordingly, our top priorities are:

Teaching AI literacy and

Mitigating the potential harm from GenAI tools currently available to the FCCPS community.

Bringing even more GenAI applications into the school system is a secondary priority. Before acting, the school should take the time necessary to be reasonably certain that there will be net benefits from adopting new GenAI tools. We see no reason for haste here, except when new GenAI applications are intended to displace more harmful GenAI tools that are already in widespread use.
